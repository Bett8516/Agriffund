SCORING METHODOLOGY

The full 100-point rubric

Climate indicators use your supplied 20-point framework. The remaining 80 points assess farm viability and repayment readiness from evidence you provide or verify.

Evaluation indicatorMaxAssessment criteria
Climate suitability & resilience · 20 pts
Rainfall suitability7Seasonal rainfall amount and distribution compared to crop needs. NASA POWER precipitation proxy; crop thresholds should be reviewed by an extension officer.
Temperature suitability5NASA POWER seasonal temperature range compared with crop/activity needs and local agronomic guidance.
Drought & extreme-weather exposure5Rainfall variability, dry-spell exposure, extreme rainfall signals. Historical climate is not a forecast.
Environmental resilience3Practical risk measures such as water harvesting, irrigation, soil cover, drainage, crop diversification; evidence reviewed.
Farm viability & repayment readiness · 80 pts
Farm plan & production evidence25Crop/activity plan (5), farm size and input budget (5), yield/production evidence (10), implementation plan (5).
Market & income outlook15Buyer / market evidence (6), price and sales records (5), income diversification (4).
Repayment capacity20Net seasonal surplus after farm costs (8), requested repayment affordability (8), cash-flow timing / other income (4).
Financial track record10Verified prior repayment or sales records (6), consistent financial records (4). No formal credit history is not an automatic penalty when alternatives are offered.
Risk buffers & safeguards10Savings/contingency buffer (4), insurance or equivalent risk sharing (3), practical household/farm contingency plan (3).
Total100Evidence quality and missing items are shown alongside the score.
Indicative bands: 80–100 strong readiness; 60–79 developing readiness; 0–59 additional evidence or support may help. Bands do not represent an approval decision or a probability of repayment.
